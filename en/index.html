About Us

We opened Selyemhaj Fodrászat in 2015 with one goal: to make sure every guest can walk out of our door feeling confident. Our team trains continuously in the latest cutting and coloring techniques, so we can always offer the freshest trends.

We believe a great hairstyle isn't just about how it looks, but also about how it makes you feel. Everyone is welcome in our friendly, relaxed salon who wants to treat themselves a little.

We look forward to welcoming you with a coffee and a good chat in our chair!
